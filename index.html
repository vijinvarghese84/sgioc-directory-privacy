SGIOC Directory Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

SGIOC Directory ("the App") is a private mobile application intended to help members of the SGIOC church community stay connected and access the church member directory.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

The SGIOC Directory may collect and store the following information provided by church members:

Member and Family Information

  • First and last names
  • Names of family and household members
  • Household and family relationships
  • Home address
  • Phone numbers
  • Email address, where applicable
  • Birthday information, excluding the year of birth
  • Wedding anniversary information, excluding the year
  • Profile photographs
  • Family photographs

Account information may also be collected to allow authorized members to securely sign in and access the directory.

2. How Information Is Collected

Information may be provided directly by members through the SGIOC Directory app or may be entered or maintained by authorized church administrators for the purpose of maintaining the church directory.

Photos are uploaded only when a member chooses to provide a profile or family photograph.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the App is used primarily to:

  • Maintain the SGIOC church directory
  • Help members identify and connect with other church members and families
  • Display member and family contact information within the authorized church community
  • Display birthdays and anniversaries without the year
  • Display profile and family photographs when provided
  • Maintain and manage user accounts
  • Provide access to the App's directory features
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the App

We do not use member information for targeted advertising or sell member information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Who Can Access Directory Information

The SGIOC Directory is intended for authorized members of the SGIOC church community.

Directory information may be visible to authenticated and authorized users of the App. Users should understand that information they or an authorized church administrator provide to the directory may therefore be visible to other authorized members.

Users should not use information obtained through the directory for purposes unrelated to the SGIOC community.

5. Photographs

Members may voluntarily upload a profile photograph or family photograph.

Uploaded photographs may be displayed to other authorized users of the SGIOC Directory as part of the church directory.

Members should only upload photographs for which they have the appropriate permission or authorization to use and share the photograph.

6. Data Storage and Service Providers

The SGIOC Directory uses third-party technology services to provide core application functionality, including secure account authentication, database storage, and storage of photographs.

Information submitted through the App may therefore be processed and stored by these service providers on behalf of SGIOC.

These services are used to operate and maintain the App and are not intended to provide member information for advertising or sale to third parties.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information stored through the SGIOC Directory against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Member information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and maintain the SGIOC church directory or as otherwise required for legitimate administrative purposes.

When information is no longer required, SGIOC may remove or delete it in accordance with its administrative practices.

9. Updating or Removing Information

Members may request that their personal information be corrected or removed from the directory.

Requests to update or remove information, including profile or family photographs, should be directed to the SGIOC church administration using the contact information provided below.

If a user requests deletion of their account, SGIOC will review the request and remove the account and associated personal information where appropriate, subject to legitimate administrative, legal, or security requirements.

10. Children's Information

The SGIOC Directory may contain information about family members who are minors because the App is a church family directory.

Parents or guardians are responsible for providing information about their children and should contact SGIOC church administration if they would like information about a minor to be corrected or removed.

The App is intended for use by authorized members of the SGIOC community and is not designed as a general-purpose children's service.

11. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services necessary to provide functionality such as authentication, database storage, file/photo storage, and other technical services.

These service providers may process information on behalf of the App solely as necessary to provide their services.

The App does not sell personal information to third parties.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at the Privacy Policy URL associated with the App.
